Privacy Policy

Application: Diploma Prep: QP & Syllabus · Last updated: September 19, 2026

Diploma Prep: QP & Syllabus (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) is developed by Baburao as a free educational resource for diploma engineering students. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No User Accounts or Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal details (such as your name, phone number, or email address) to use any feature of this app.
  • No Device Permissions: The app does not request access to sensitive device features such as your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or external storage.

2. Network & Technical Data

To provide study materials and in-app viewing:

  • Internet Connection: The app requires Internet access (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) solely to load educational question papers, notes, and syllabus documents hosted on Cloudflare R2 storage via an in-app viewer.
  • Anonymous Document Feedback: When you tap the feedback buttons (helpful / not helpful), the app transmits an anonymous vote containing only the document title, category, and vote type (UP or DOWN) to our feedback sheet. No personal identification, IP address, or device fingerprint is associated with your vote.
  • Local Preferences: Your bookmarks and voted document states are saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes trusted standard infrastructure services:

  • Cloudflare R2: Used for secure, high-speed hosting and streaming of static educational PDF documents.
  • Google Docs Viewer (in WebView): Used strictly for rendering static PDF files safely inside the application.
  • Google Play Services: Standard Google Play services that facilitate app stability, performance monitoring, and secure distribution via the Google Play Store.

These providers process standard network transmission data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy →

4. Security & Content Protection

We take the security of our educational resources and user experience seriously:

  • The in-app document viewer utilizes standard Android security flags (FLAG_SECURE) to prevent unauthorized screen captures and protect user privacy while studying.
  • The app does not download executable code (APK, DEX, or JAR files) and is fully compliant with Google Play's Device and Network Abuse policies.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app is an educational study guide designed for polytechnic and diploma engineering students. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
