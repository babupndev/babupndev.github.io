Privacy Policy

Application: AU NSW DKT 2026 · Package: com.baburao.nswdkt · Last updated: 17 September 2026

AU NSW DKT 2026 is an independent educational application developed by Baburao to help learners prepare for the NSW Driver Knowledge Test. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorised by Transport for NSW, Service NSW, or the NSW Government.

1. Information We Collect

AU NSW DKT 2026 is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information to provide the core study experience:

  • Profile data (stored locally on your device): name, age bracket, gender, country of origin, and study goal that you voluntarily enter during setup.
  • Study progress data (stored locally on your device): question history, topic progress, mock test results, mistake bank entries, and study streak.
  • Device & usage analytics: anonymised crash reports and app usage events collected via Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Advertising identifiers: used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your personalised study statistics and progress within the app.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

3. Data Storage

All personal profile data and study progress is stored exclusively on your local device using Android DataStore and SQLite (Room). This data is never transmitted to our servers. You have full control over this data at all times.

4. Third-Party Services

This app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase Analytics – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob – policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertising

AU NSW DKT 2026 displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show personalised ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for users aged 16 and above (learner driver age in NSW). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can permanently erase all locally stored study data at any time directly within the app:

  • Open the app → Profile & Settings → Reset Practice Progress

To remove your device from Firebase Analytics tracking, you can disable analytics in your device's Google settings.

8. Security

Local data is stored using Android's encrypted storage mechanisms. We follow industry-standard security practices. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
